# Exchange logos

HTX's logo (`htx.png`) is its official app icon, from htx.com (`/pwa/icon-pwa-512.png`), scaled down. Bitfinex's (`bitfinex.png`) and CoinDCX's (`coindcx.png`) are their official iOS app icons, from the App Store (512 px, scaled down to 128). AscendEX's (`ascendex.svg`) is its logo as CoinGecko shows it, on a white square. CoinList's (`coinlist.svg`) is its official mark, from coinlist.co (`/icon.svg`), in white on a dark square.
